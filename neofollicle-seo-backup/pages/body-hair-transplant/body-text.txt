When the Scalp Can’t Donate—The Body Steps In Body hair transplant (BHT) uses hairs from areas like the beard, chest, or back to fill in scalp baldness, especially when scalp donor supply is limited. In a body hair transplant, grafts are taken from areas such as the beard, chest, shoulders, or back and implanted into balding areas on the scalp. It’s especially helpful for patients with advanced hair loss, poor scalp donor density, or those needing repair procedures after failed transplants.The technique is performed using FUE, ensuring minimal scarring and natural-looking results. Yes, especially beard and chest hair. While body hair differs slightly in texture, it can improve scalp coverage significantly when done right. Where can body hair be taken from? Common donor areas include the beard, chest, shoulders, and back. We select areas with dense, strong follicles. Will the results look natural? Absolutely—when done by an expert. We blend body and scalp hair strategically for a smooth, natural look. Can body hair be used for full baldness? It’s ideal for crown, mid-scalp, and density enhancement. Hairline design usually uses scalp or beard grafts for a natural finish. Is the procedure safe and permanent? Yes. The transplanted hair grows permanently.
