## FAQs – Scalp Micropigmentation in Bangalore

### Will it look like real hair?

Yes! When done correctly, SMP gives the illusion of natural hair follicles, blending in with your existing hair or giving you the look of a closely shaved head.

### How long does scalp micropigmentation last?

SMP results typically last 4–6 years, depending on your skin type, sun exposure, and aftercare. Occasional touch-ups may be needed to maintain sharpness.

### Is SMP painful?

It feels like light tapping or scratching. Most people rate the discomfort as 2–3 out of 10, and numbing cream can be used if needed.

### How many sessions do I need?

Most clients need 2–3 sessions, spaced 7–14 days apart, to build up pigment layers and achieve the desired look.

### Is there any downtime after the procedure?

No major downtime! You may see slight redness for 1–2 days, but you can resume normal activities the same day.

### Can SMP work for women too?

Absolutely. SMP is ideal for women with visible scalp or thinning hair, as it reduces the contrast between hair and scalp, making hair look denser.

### Does SMP cause any damage to the scalp or hair?

Not at all. It’s a superficial skin treatment, and it won’t affect your natural hair growth or cause any hair loss.

### Can I combine SMP with hair transplant or PRP?

Yes! Many patients use SMP to enhance hair transplant results, cover scars, or create the illusion of fullness between PRP sessions.
