Types of Hair Transplant Techniques

Hair transplant techniques differ in how grafts are extracted, how recipient sites are prepared, and how follicles are implanted. At Neo Follicle, the right technique is recommended after assessing the donor area, baldness grade, scalp condition, graft requirement, and long-term hair growth plan.
Patients often come across terms like FUE, FUT, Sapphire FUE, Direct Follicle Implantation, and NFT Method while researching hair transplant options. These techniques are not all the same. Some describe how grafts are extracted, while others describe how the follicles are implanted.

The best technique depends on the patient’s hair loss pattern, donor area strength, expected density, cosmetic goals, and suitability after medical evaluation.

FUE Hair Transplant

FUE, or Follicular Unit Extraction, is a technique where individual hair follicles are extracted from the donor area and implanted into thinning or bald areas.

Best for: Patients looking for individual graft extraction, minimal visible donor scarring, and faster recovery compared to strip surgery.

Key benefit: No linear scar and suitable for hairline, crown, beard, eyebrow, and repair cases.

FUT Hair Transplant

FUT, or Follicular Unit Transplantation, is a strip-based technique where a thin strip of scalp is removed from the donor area and grafts are separated for implantation.

Best for: Selected patients who need a larger number of grafts and have a suitable donor area.

Key benefit: Can provide higher graft yield in selected cases.

Direct Hair Implantation

Direct Hair Implantation is an implanter-assisted technique where extracted grafts are placed directly into the recipient area with control over angle, depth, direction, and density.

Best for: Patients who need precise implantation, natural hairline refinement, or controlled density in selected areas.

Key benefit: Supports accurate graft placement and natural hair direction.

Sapphire FUE Hair Transplant

Sapphire FUE is a variation of FUE where sapphire blades are used to create fine recipient channels before graft implantation.

Best for: Patients who need refined recipient site creation, planned density, and natural-looking implantation.

Key benefit: Supports precise channel creation, angle planning, and direction control.

NFT Method by Neo Follicle

NFT Method is Neo Follicle’s signature direct implantation approach using Neo Implanters for controlled graft placement with reduced graft handling.

Best for: Patients looking for natural-looking results, density planning, donor area protection, and refined implanter-assisted hair restoration.

Key benefit: Helps control angle, depth, direction, and density while supporting graft protection.

Robotic Hair Transplant

Robotic Hair Transplant uses computer-assisted technology to help identify, select, and extract suitable hair follicles from the donor area with precision.

Best for: Selected patients who are suitable for technology-assisted follicle extraction and want a highly planned donor harvesting process.

Key benefit:Supports precision in graft selection and extraction, but the final result still depends on hairline design, graft handling, implantation quality, and surgeon planning.

Hair Transplant: Basics You Need to Know

If you're considering a hair transplant in Bangalore, you’re probably curious about what the procedure involves and how it can help restore your hair. We’re here to break it down for you in simple, easy-to-understand terms. Let’s dive into the basics!

A hair transplant is a procedure where hair follicles from one part of your scalp (usually the back or sides) are moved to areas where hair is thinning or balding. Think of it like relocating healthy, strong hair to areas where your hair is struggling. Over time, these transplanted hairs start growing in their new spots, giving you a fuller, more natural look.

A hair follicle is a tiny structure in the skin that grows hair. It contains cells that produce keratin, the protein that forms hair strands. Each follicle goes through a growth cycle, including growth, rest, and shedding.

A hair graft refers to a group of hair follicles that are transplanted from the donor area to the recipient area during a hair transplant procedure. A graft typically contains multiple follicles, usually 1 to 4, grouped together.

While a follicle is the individual structure that grows hair, a graft is the unit of hair follicles that are moved during a transplant. The success of a hair transplant relies on the survival and growth of the grafts once transplanted.

FAQs on Hair Transplant in Bangalore

Still wondering if hair restoration is right for you? Here are clear answers from the Best Hair Transplant Clinic in Bangalore to help you make an informed decision.

Direct hair transplant is a hair restoration approach where healthy hair follicles are extracted from the donor area and implanted into thinning or bald areas with careful control over angle, depth, direction, and density. The goal is to create natural-looking hair growth while protecting the donor area.

The NFT Method is Neo Follicle’s signature direct follicle implantation approach. It uses European Neo Implanters to place harvested grafts with control over angle, depth, direction, and density. The method is designed to reduce graft handling, support natural-looking results, and protect graft quality.

There is no single best technique for every patient. FUE and FUT describe graft extraction methods, Sapphire FUE refers to recipient channel creation, and NFT Method is Neo Follicle’s implanter-assisted direct placement approach. The right technique depends on donor area strength, baldness grade, scalp condition, graft requirement, and expected density.

Direct hair transplant may be suitable for men and women with visible hair loss, receding hairline, crown baldness, frontal thinning, stable pattern hair loss, good donor area, and realistic expectations. Suitability is confirmed after scalp evaluation and donor assessment.

Patients with weak donor area, unstable active hair fall, diffuse thinning, active scalp infection, uncontrolled medical conditions, or unrealistic density expectations may need further evaluation. Some patients may need medical treatment before considering surgery.

The cost of direct hair transplant in Bangalore depends on the number of grafts required, baldness grade, donor area quality, treatment area, technique used, and complexity of the case. At Neo Follicle, a personalised cost estimate is given after consultation and graft planning.

Direct hair transplant is usually performed under local anaesthesia. Patients may feel mild pressure or discomfort during some stages, but significant pain is usually avoided. Mild soreness, swelling, or tightness can occur after the procedure and usually settles gradually.

Most patients experience mild redness, scabbing, or swelling during the first few days. Many can return to routine non-strenuous work within a few days, depending on the procedure size and comfort level. The clinic provides personalised post-care instructions.

Hair transplant results develop gradually. Some shedding may occur in the first few weeks. Early growth may begin around 3 to 4 months, visible improvement is commonly seen around 6 months, and fuller results usually develop over 9 to 12 months.

Transplanted hair is usually taken from the safe donor area, which is more resistant to pattern hair loss. However, existing non-transplanted hair may continue to thin over time. Long-term hair loss management may be needed to preserve overall density.

Hair transplants are generally safe, but like any procedure, they carry some risks. These may include temporary swelling, redness, mild pain, itching, or infection (rare if done in hygienic conditions). These side effects are usually minor and subside within a few days to weeks.
