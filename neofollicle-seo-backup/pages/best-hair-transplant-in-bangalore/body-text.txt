Direct hair transplant is a modern hair restoration approach where healthy hair follicles are extracted from the donor area and implanted into thinning or bald areas with careful control over angle, depth, direction, and density. At Neo Follicle Transplant Clinic, the procedure is planned to create a natural-looking hairline, protect the donor area, support graft survival, and achieve long-term hair growth. Is direct hair transplant the same as FUE? Not exactly. FUE mainly describes how grafts are extracted from the donor area. Direct hair transplant focuses more on how the grafts are placed into the recipient area with controlled angle, direction, depth, and density.In many cases, FUE extraction may be combined with an implanter-assisted or direct implantation approach. Why do angle and direction matter? Hair does not grow straight out of the scalp. It grows at a natural angle and direction depending on the area of the scalp. If grafts are placed incorrectly, the result may look artificial.Proper angle and direction help the transplanted hair blend naturally with existing hair. Why does graft handling matter? Hair follicles are delicate. Excessive handling, drying, trauma, or long out-of-body time can affect graft quality. A good hair transplant workflow focuses on protecting the grafts from extraction to implantation. Does direct hair transplant give immediate results? No. The implanted hair may shed initially, which is usually part of the normal growth cycle. New growth starts gradually over the next few months, and fuller results commonly develop over 9 to 12 months. Understand Your Hair Transplant Plan. FUE Hair Transplant FUE, or Follicular Unit Extraction, is a technique where individual hair follicles are extracted from the donor area and implanted into thinning or bald areas. Best for: Patients looking for individual graft extraction, minimal visible donor scarring, and faster recovery compared to strip surgery. Key benefit: No linear scar and suitable for hairline, crown, beard, eyebrow, and repair cases. FUT Hair Transplant FUT, or Follicular Unit Transplantation, is a strip-based technique where a thin strip of scalp is removed from the donor area and grafts are separated for implantation. Best for: Selected patients who need a larger number of grafts and have a suitable donor area. Key benefit: Can provide higher graft yield in selected cases. Direct Hair Implantation Direct Hair Implantation is an implanter-assisted technique where extracted grafts are placed directly into the recipient area with control over angle, depth, direction, and density. Best for: Patients who need precise implantation, natural hairline refinement, or controlled density in selected areas. Key benefit: Supports accurate graft placement and natural hair direction. Sapphire FUE Hair Transplant Sapphire FUE is a variation of FUE where sapphire blades are used to create fine recipient channels before graft implantation. Best for: Patients who need refined recipient site creation, planned density, and natural-looking implantation. Key benefit: Supports precise channel creation, angle planning, and direction control. NFT Method by Neo Follicle NFT Method is Neo Follicle’s signature direct implantation approach using Neo Implanters for controlled graft placement with reduced graft handling. Best for: Patients looking for natural-looking results, density planning, donor area protection, and refined implanter-assisted hair restoration. Key benefit: Helps control angle, depth, direction, and density while supporting graft protection. Robotic Hair Transplant Robotic Hair Transplant uses computer-assisted technology to help identify, select, and extract suitable hair follicles from the donor area with precision. Best for: Selected patients who are suitable for technology-assisted follicle extraction and want a highly planned donor harvesting process. Key benefit:Supports precision in graft selection and extraction, but the final result still depends on hairline design, graft handling, implantation quality, and surgeon planning. We’re here to break it down for you in simple, easy-to-understand terms. Let’s dive into the basics! What is a Hair Transplant? A hair transplant is a procedure where hair follicles from one part of your scalp (usually the back or sides) are moved to areas where hair is thinning or balding. Think of it like relocating healthy, strong hair to areas where your hair is struggling. Over time, these transplanted hairs start growing in their new spots, giving you a fuller, more natural look. What is a Follicle? A hair follicle is a tiny structure in the skin that grows hair. It contains cells that produce keratin, the protein that forms hair strands. Each follicle goes through a growth cycle, including growth, rest, and shedding. What is a Graft? A hair graft refers to a group of hair follicles that are transplanted from the donor area to the recipient area during a hair transplant procedure. A graft typically contains multiple follicles, usually 1 to 4, grouped together.While a follicle is the individual structure that grows hair, a graft is the unit of hair follicles that are moved during a transplant. The success of a hair transplant relies on the survival and growth of the grafts once transplanted. Click below button to Call Now for Hair Transplant in Bangalore.
