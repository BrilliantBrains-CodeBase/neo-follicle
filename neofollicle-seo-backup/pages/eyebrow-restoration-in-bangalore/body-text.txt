Here are clear answers from the Best Eyebrow Transplant Clinic in Bangalore to help you make an informed decision. Will the transplanted eyebrow hair look natural? Yes, absolutely. We match the angle and direction of your natural brows so that they blend seamlessly. Can I shape or trim my new eyebrows? Yes. Once fully grown, you can maintain them like normal brows—including trimming or shaping. How long does the eyebrow restoration procedure take? The procedure typically takes 2 to 4 hours, depending on how many grafts are needed and whether you’re getting one or both eyebrows restored. It’s done in a single session and you can return home the same day. Is there any pain or downtime? The procedure is painless, and you can resume most normal activities the next day. How long do the results last? Yes, the results are permanent. Once the transplanted hair follicles take root and grow, they continue to behave like your natural hair. Is this better than microblading or eyebrow tattooing? Yes—because the hair is real, soft, and grows naturally. No fading, no artificial look. Can I wear makeup after the procedure? You should avoid makeup on or around the eyebrow area for about 7–10 days after the procedure to allow proper healing. After that, you can safely use makeup as usual. What’s the difference between full and partial eyebrow restoration? Full restoration involves rebuilding the entire eyebrow, while partial restoration targets specific areas with gaps, thinning, or scarring.
