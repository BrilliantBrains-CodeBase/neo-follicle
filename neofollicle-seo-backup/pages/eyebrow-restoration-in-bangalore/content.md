## What is Eyebrow Restoration?

#### A Simple, Permanent Fix for Thin or Damaged Eyebrows

From over-plucking to hair loss conditions, eyebrow restoration helps bring your brows back—better than before.

![Indian woman glowing skin](https://neofollicletransplant.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Indian-Woman-glowing-skin.jpg)

Eyebrow restoration is a minimally invasive procedure where healthy hair is taken from the back of your scalp and carefully implanted into your eyebrow area. Each hair is placed with attention to angle, direction, and density—so the final look is **natural, symmetrical, and soft.**

This treatment is ideal for people with:

- Over-plucked or naturally sparse brows

- Scars or gaps in the eyebrow area

- Hair loss from trauma, burns, or medical treatments

- A desire for thicker, better-shaped eyebrows without makeup or tattooing

## FAQs on Eyebrow Transplant in Bangalore

Still wondering if Eyebrow hair restoration is right for you? Here are clear answers from the Best Eyebrow Transplant Clinic in Bangalore to help you make an informed decision.

### Will the transplanted eyebrow hair look natural?

Yes, absolutely. We match the angle and direction of your natural brows so that they blend seamlessly.

### Can I shape or trim my new eyebrows?

Yes. Once fully grown, you can maintain them like normal brows—including trimming or shaping.

### How long does the eyebrow restoration procedure take?

The procedure typically takes 2 to 4 hours, depending on how many grafts are needed and whether you’re getting one or both eyebrows restored. It’s done in a single session and you can return home the same day.

### Is there any pain or downtime?

The procedure is painless, and you can resume most normal activities the next day.

### How long do the results last?

Yes, the results are permanent. Once the transplanted hair follicles take root and grow, they continue to behave like your natural hair.

### Is this better than microblading or eyebrow tattooing?

Yes—because the hair is real, soft, and grows naturally. No fading, no artificial look.

### Can I wear makeup after the procedure?

You should avoid makeup on or around the eyebrow area for about 7–10 days after the procedure to allow proper healing. After that, you can safely use makeup as usual.

### What’s the difference between full and partial eyebrow restoration?

Full restoration involves rebuilding the entire eyebrow, while partial restoration targets specific areas with gaps, thinning, or scarring. We’ll determine the best approach based on your current brow condition and goals.
