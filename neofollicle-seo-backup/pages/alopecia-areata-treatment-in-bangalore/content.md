Alopecia Areata is an autoimmune condition that causes patchy hair loss—often suddenly and without warning.

Alopecia Areata occurs when the immune system mistakenly attacks hair follicles, causing round or oval patches of hair loss on the scalp, beard, eyebrows, or other body parts. It affects both men and women and can happen at any age.

## FAQs – Alopecia Areata Treatment in Bangalore

### What causes alopecia areata?

Alopecia Areata is an autoimmune condition where your immune system mistakenly attacks hair follicles. This causes sudden patchy hair loss, though the exact trigger can vary from stress to genetic factors.

### Is alopecia areata curable?

There is no permanent cure, but it is highly treatable. Many patients experience full regrowth with the right combination of therapies and early intervention.

### How long does it take to see hair regrowth?

Visible regrowth often begins within 4–8 weeks of starting treatment. However, full restoration may take a few months depending on the size and number of patches.

### Can alopecia areata return after treatment?

Yes, it can recur. That’s why we focus on both treatment and long-term maintenance, including immune modulation and stress management.

### Is the treatment painful?

Most treatments like PRP or steroid injections cause minimal discomfort, and we use numbing creams when necessary to keep it comfortable.

### Can children get treated for alopecia areata?

Yes, our clinic provides safe and gentle treatment plans for children and teens, tailored to their age and hair health.

### What’s the difference between alopecia areata and regular hair fall?

Alopecia causes round, smooth bald patches, often suddenly, while regular hair fall is more gradual and diffused. Diagnosis through scalp analysis helps confirm the condition.

### Can alopecia areata spread to the whole scalp?

In rare cases, yes—this is called Alopecia Totalis. Early treatment reduces the risk of progression and improves regrowth chances significantly.
