## What is Exosome Hair Treatment?

#### Small Messengers. Big Results. Hair Growth Backed by Cell Science

Exosome therapy is a cutting-edge treatment that uses tiny vesicles called exosomes. Exosomes are tiny, cell-derived vesicles that tell your hair follicles to grow stronger, thicker, and faster.

These exosomes aid cell communication and repair. They contain growth factors and proteins that stimulate hair follicles, promoting new hair growth. Unlike traditional treatments, exosome hair therapy tackles hair loss’s root cause. It boosts the scalp and hair follicles’ natural healing processes.

Exosome therapy is one of the most advanced forms of regenerative medicine for hair loss. It uses growth-factor-rich vesicles, extracted from stem cells, to stimulate dormant or weakened hair follicles.

Unlike PRP, exosomes don’t rely on your own blood—they come ready-made, packed with powerful signaling proteins that promote healing, regeneration, and new hair growth.

It's ideal for people who want a next-level solution without surgery or long downtime.

## FAQs – Exosome Hair Treatment in Bangalore

### What exactly are exosomes?

Exosomes are tiny cell messengers derived from stem cells. They carry proteins and growth signals that help repair and regenerate hair follicles—making them ideal for stimulating new hair growth.

### How is Exosome different from PRP or GFC?

Unlike PRP or GFC, exosomes come pre-prepared and contain higher concentrations of growth factors. There’s no need to draw your blood, and results are often faster and more predictable.

### Is the Exosome treatment safe?

Yes. The exosome solution is lab-processed, purified, and rigorously tested for safety. It’s widely used in regenerative medicine and cosmetic treatments with minimal risk.

### How many sessions will I need?

Most people require 1 to 3 sessions, spaced 3–4 weeks apart. The number of sessions depends on your scalp condition and desired outcome.

### When will I see visible results?

You may notice reduced hair fall and better hair texture in about 4–6 weeks. New hair growth generally becomes visible in 2–3 months.

### Does Exosome Therapy hurt?

The injections are very fine and cause minimal discomfort. We can apply a numbing cream to make the experience more comfortable.

### Is iExosome Therapy suitable for women too?

Absolutely! Exosome therapy is ideal for female pattern hair loss, postpartum shedding, or thinning from hormonal changes.

### Can I combine Exosome therapy with PRP or other treatments?

Yes! Exosome therapy can be used on its own or combined with PRP, GFC, or post-transplant therapy for enhanced results.
