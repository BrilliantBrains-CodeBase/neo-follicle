Exosomes are tiny cell messengers derived from stem cells. They carry proteins and growth signals that help repair and regenerate hair follicles—making them ideal for stimulating new hair growth. How is Exosome different from PRP or GFC? Unlike PRP or GFC, exosomes come pre-prepared and contain higher concentrations of growth factors. There’s no need to draw your blood, and results are often faster and more predictable. Is the Exosome treatment safe? Yes. The exosome solution is lab-processed, purified, and rigorously tested for safety. It’s widely used in regenerative medicine and cosmetic treatments with minimal risk. How many sessions will I need? Most people require 1 to 3 sessions, spaced 3–4 weeks apart. The number of sessions depends on your scalp condition and desired outcome. When will I see visible results? You may notice reduced hair fall and better hair texture in about 4–6 weeks. New hair growth generally becomes visible in 2–3 months. Does Exosome Therapy hurt? The injections are very fine and cause minimal discomfort. We can apply a numbing cream to make the experience more comfortable. Is iExosome Therapy suitable for women too? Absolutely! Exosome therapy is ideal for female pattern hair loss, postpartum shedding, or thinning from hormonal changes. Can I combine Exosome therapy with PRP or other treatments? Yes!
