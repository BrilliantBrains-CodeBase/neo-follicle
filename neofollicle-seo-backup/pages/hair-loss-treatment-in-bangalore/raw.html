Why Hair Loss Should Be Diagnosed Before Treatment

Hair Loss Treatment Starts with Finding the Cause

Not all hair loss is the same. Some patients experience gradual thinning due to androgenetic alopecia, while others develop sudden hair fall due to stress, illness, nutritional deficiency, hormonal imbalance, dandruff, autoimmune conditions, or scalp inflammation. That is why the first step in treatment is not simply choosing PRP, GFC, QR678, or hair transplant. The first step is a proper diagnosis.

Advanced Hair Loss Treatments Available in Bangalore

PRP Hair Treatment in Bangalore

PRP, or Platelet-Rich Plasma therapy, uses the patient’s own blood-derived growth factors to support weak hair follicles and improve hair thickness in selected cases. It is commonly used for early-stage hair thinning and as a supportive treatment after hair transplant.

Best suited for: Early hair thinning, weak follicles, diffuse hair fall, maintenance therapy.

Learn more about PRP Hair Treatment in Bangalore

GFC Hair Treatment in Bangalore

GFC, or Growth Factor Concentrate therapy, is an advanced regenerative hair treatment where concentrated growth factors are prepared from the patient’s blood and injected into the scalp. It is designed to support hair follicle activity and improve hair density in suitable patients.

Best suited for: Hair thinning, early hair loss, patients looking for a more concentrated growth factor treatment.

Learn more about GFC Hair Treatment in Bangalore

QR678 Hair Treatment in Bangalore

QR678 is a non-surgical hair regrowth treatment that uses a specific formulation of growth factors to support hair follicle function. It may be considered for patients with early to moderate hair loss depending on their diagnosis and suitability.

Best suited for: Androgenetic alopecia, thinning hair, early-stage hair loss.

Exosome Hair Treatment in Bangalore

Exosome therapy is an advanced hair restoration approach that uses cell-signaling components to support scalp and follicle health. It is often positioned as an advanced regenerative option for selected patients with hair thinning or poor follicular activity.

Best suited for: Patients seeking advanced regenerative hair restoration options after dermatologist assessment.

Stem Cell Therapy for Hair Loss in Bangalore

Stem cell-based hair restoration treatments aim to support scalp repair, follicle stimulation, and hair regrowth potential in suitable cases. The treatment suitability depends on the patient’s stage of hair loss, follicle condition, age, and diagnosis.

Do You Need Hair Regrowth Treatment or Hair Transplant?

Many patients are confused about whether they need PRP, GFC, QR678, exosomes, laser therapy, or a hair transplant. The answer depends on whether the hair follicles are still active.

If the follicles are weak but still present, non-surgical treatments may help improve thickness, density, and scalp health. If the follicles are permanently lost in bald areas, hair transplant may be a better option.

Frequently Asked Questions

Still wondering if hair restoration is right for you? Here are clear answers from the Best Hair Transplant Clinic in Bangalore to help you make an informed decision.

Which is the best hair loss treatment in Bangalore?

The best hair loss treatment depends on the cause and stage of hair loss. PRP, GFC, QR678, exosomes, stem cell therapy, LLLT, medications, dandruff treatment, alopecia treatment, SMP, or hair transplant may be recommended after proper diagnosis.

Can hair loss be treated without hair transplant?

Yes. Early-stage hair loss, thinning, dandruff-related hair fall, alopecia areata, and weak follicles may be managed with non-surgical treatments. However, if follicles are permanently lost in bald areas, hair transplant may be required.

Is PRP better or GFC better for hair loss?

Both PRP and GFC are regenerative treatments, but they are prepared differently. The right choice depends on your hair loss pattern, follicle health, previous treatment history, and doctor’s assessment.

Does QR678 help in hair regrowth?

QR678 may help selected patients with early to moderate hair loss. It is important to consult a qualified hair loss doctor to understand whether you are suitable for this treatment.

Is exosome therapy good for hair loss?

Exosome therapy is an advanced regenerative treatment option. It may be considered for selected patients after evaluating the scalp, follicle condition, and stage of hair loss.

Can dandruff cause hair fall?

Dandruff can contribute to itching, inflammation, scratching, and scalp irritation, which may worsen hair fall. Treating dandruff medically can improve scalp health and support better hair growth conditions.

What is the difference between alopecia and normal hair fall?

Normal hair fall usually appears as diffuse shedding, while alopecia can refer to different types of hair loss, including patchy hair loss, autoimmune hair loss, or pattern baldness. A dermatologist can help identify the exact type.

When is hair transplant needed?

Hair transplant may be needed when hair follicles are permanently lost in bald areas and non-surgical treatments are unlikely to regrow hair. It is usually considered for advanced male or female pattern baldness.

Is scalp micropigmentation a hair regrowth treatment?

No. Scalp micropigmentation does not regrow hair. It is a cosmetic procedure that creates the appearance of better density or a shaved scalp look using micro-pigmentation.

How do I know which hair loss treatment is right for me?

The right treatment can be decided only after evaluating your hair loss pattern, scalp condition, medical history, hair density, and follicle health. A consultation with a dermatologist or hair transplant surgeon is the best first step.
