#### Hair Restoration That Fits Your Lifestyle

Unlike traditional methods, this technique restores your hair without fully shaving your scalp—so you can get treated without anyone knowing.

## FAQs – Unshaven Hair Transplant in Bangalore

### Will I have to shave my entire head?

No, that’s the point! We either trim a very small donor patch or use completely unshaven FUE depending on your case.

### 2\. How is unshaven different from regular FUE?

It’s the same FUE method, but more discreet—designed for patients who don’t want a visible haircut.

### 3\. Will the results be as natural as normal transplants?

Yes. With proper planning and an experienced surgeon, the results are just as natural and permanent.

### 4\. Can women opt for this procedure?

Absolutely. In fact, unshaven transplant is especially popular among female patients.

### 5\. How long is the recovery time?

There’s no visible recovery. Most patients resume work in 1–2 days without anyone noticing they had a procedure.

### 6\. How many grafts can be done in an unshaven transplant?

Up to 2,000–2,500 grafts are possible in one session, depending on donor availability and area covered.

### Is it more expensive than normal FUE?

Slightly—due to longer operating time and higher surgical skill—but still very affordable compared to international rates.

### Can I combine this with PRP or GFC therapy?

Yes, many patients combine unshaven transplant with PRP or GFC to improve density and healing.
