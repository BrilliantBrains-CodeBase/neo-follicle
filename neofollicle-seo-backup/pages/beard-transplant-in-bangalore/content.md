#### 1\. What is a beard transplant?

A beard transplant is a facial hair restoration procedure where hair follicles are taken from the donor area and implanted into patchy or thin beard areas.

#### 2\. Who is suitable for beard transplant?

Men with patchy beard, absent beard, thin moustache, uneven goatee, scar-related beard loss, or genetically poor facial hair growth may be suitable after doctor evaluation.

#### 3\. Is beard transplant painful?

Not at all. The procedure is done under local anesthesia, so you’ll be fully awake but won’t feel any pain. Most patients describe it as a comfortable experience and are even surprised by how quick and easy it feels.

#### 4\. When will I see beard transplant results?

Initial growth begins around 3–4 months after the procedure. You’ll see the full beard density and shape take form by 9–12 months. And yes, the results are permanent.

#### 5\. Can I shave after beard transplant?

Yes! Once your new beard has fully grown in (usually after 6–8 months), you can trim, shape, shave, or style it however you like—just like natural facial hair. The clinic will give specific instruction on when to shave.

#### 6\. Will transplanted beard hair look natural?

Yes, 100%. We carefully design and place each hair to match your natural growth direction and density. Once fully grown, it blends seamlessly with your existing facial hair. No one will know—it just looks like you always had it.

#### 7\. How much does beard transplant cost in Bangalore?

The cost depends on the graft requirement, treated area, density goals, and complexity of the case. A consultation is needed for an accurate estimate.
