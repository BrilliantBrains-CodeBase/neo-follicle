How is hair transplant cost calculated? Hair transplant cost is usually calculated based on the number of grafts required. The basic formula is: Total Cost = Number of Grafts Required × Cost Per Graft For example, if a patient needs 2000 grafts, the total cost will depend on the per-graft rate recommended after consultation. 3. What is the cost of 1000 grafts in Bangalore? At a price range of ₹60 to ₹100 per graft, 1000 grafts may cost approximately ₹60,000 to ₹1,00,000. At a price range of ₹60 to ₹100 per graft, 3000 grafts may cost approximately ₹1,80,000 to ₹3,00,000. This may usually fall under a larger session depending on your graft requirement and follicle coverage needs. 6. How many follicles are there in one graft? One graft may contain more than one follicle. At Neo Follicle Transplant Clinic, session ranges are explained using both grafts and follicles. For example, 1000–1500 grafts may approximately contain 2000–3000 follicles, depending on the patient’s natural follicle grouping. 7. Why does hair transplant cost vary from person to person? Cost varies because each patient has a different baldness grade, graft requirement, donor area strength, follicle density, scalp condition, and density expectation. Two patients may need different plans even if their baldness looks similar from the outside. 8. Is the lowest cost per graft always better? No. The lowest cost per graft should not be the only deciding factor. A safe and natural-looking hair transplant depends on proper graft planning, donor area protection, follicle handling, hairline design, implantation angle, and doctor supervision. 9. What is included in the hair transplant cost? The cost usually includes doctor consultation, scalp assessment, donor area evaluation, graft requirement planning, hairline design, procedure charges, local anaesthesia, post-procedure instructions, and basic follow-up guidance. Exact inclusions are explained during consultation. 10. What is not included in the hair transplant cost? Some items may be charged separately, such as blood tests, long-term medicines, PRP, GFC, QR678, exosome therapy, additional sessions, corrective procedures, travel, stay, or special medical clearance if required. 11. Will I need PRP or other therapies after hair transplant? Not every patient needs additional therapies. Is hair transplant a one-time cost? For many patients, transplanted grafts can provide long-lasting coverage. However, existing non-transplanted follicles may continue to thin over time.
