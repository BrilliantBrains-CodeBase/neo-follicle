QR678 contains a mix of natural growth factors and peptides that are already present in your body. It’s clinically tested, FDA-approved in India, and US-patented. It’s widely used with no reported long-term side effects. How many sessions will I need? Most patients need around 6 to 8 sessions, spaced 2–3 weeks apart, to see noticeable results. Maintenance sessions may be suggested every 6–8 months. Does the treatment hurt? Can I combine QR678 with PRP or other treatments? Yes, QR678 can be used along with PRP, stem cell therapy, or even post-hair transplant to enhance results and support follicle health.
