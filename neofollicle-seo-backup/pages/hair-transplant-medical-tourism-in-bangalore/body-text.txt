FUE (Follicular Unit Extraction) A minimally invasive technique where individual hair follicles are extracted from the donor area and implanted into bald spots. No stitches, no visible scarring, and faster recovery. Ideal for natural-looking, long-lasting hair restoration. FUT (Follicular Unit Transplantation) Involves removing a thin strip of scalp from the back of the head and dissecting it into grafts. Best for covering large bald areas in a single session. May leave a linear scar, but ensures high graft yield. Unshaven Hair Transplant Hair transplant without shaving the head—perfect for professionals or those seeking discretion. Only the donor area is trimmed slightly, leaving existing hair untouched. Ideal for mild to moderate baldness in visible areas. Beard & Eyebrow Transplant Restores patchy or missing facial hair using hair from the scalp. Carefully placed grafts match the natural growth direction and density. Great for fuller beards, sideburns, or perfectly shaped brows. Female Hairline Correction Designed to refine high or uneven hairlines in women using precision graft placement. Achieves a softer, more balanced look that enhances facial aesthetics. Safe, effective, and tailored for female hair patterns. Body Hair Transplant Uses hair from the chest, beard, or other body areas when scalp donor hair is limited. A useful option for repair cases or high-grade baldness. Helps increase coverage and density in targeted zones. PRP / GFC / Stem Cell Therapies (Add-ons) Boost your transplant results with growth factor–rich therapies. These add-ons improve healing, reduce shedding, and stimulate stronger regrowth. Often used before or after transplant for maximum effect. Explore All Hair Restoration Procedures.
