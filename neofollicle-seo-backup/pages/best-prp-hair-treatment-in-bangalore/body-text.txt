Yes, it’s completely safe. PRP uses your own blood, so there’s no risk of allergies or side effects. It’s a natural, low-risk way to stimulate hair growth. How many sessions will I need for visible results? Most people see results after 3 to 4 sessions, spaced about 3–4 weeks apart. Some may require maintenance sessions once every 6–12 months. Does PRP hair treatment hurt? You might feel slight pinching or pressure during the injections, but it’s not considered painful. Numbing cream can be used if needed. When will I start seeing results? You may see reduced hair fall in 3–4 weeks, and visible hair regrowth in 2–3 months, with continued improvement in thickness and texture over time. When will I start seeing new hair growth? You may notice reduced hair fall within a few weeks. Visible hair regrowth and improvement in thickness usually appear after 2–3 months. Can PRP help with bald spots? PRP works best on thinning areas with active follicles. It may not be very effective in completely bald areas—those may need a hair transplant instead. Are there any side effects of PRP therapy? Mild redness or tenderness in the scalp may occur, but this usually subsides in a few hours. Since it’s your own blood, the treatment is generally very safe. Is PRP suitable for women? Absolutely! Many women benefit from PRP, especially those with postpartum hair loss, hormonal changes, or general thinning. Can I wash or style my hair after PRP? You can wash your hair after 24 hours and resume normal styling. Just avoid chemical treatments for a few days after each session. Have more questions? Let’s talk one-on-one.
