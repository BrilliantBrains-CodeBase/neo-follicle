## What is PRP Hair Treatment?

#### A Natural Boost for Your Hair—No Pills or Transplants Needed

PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) therapy uses your own blood to stimulate hair growth and reduce hair fall—safely and naturally.

Play

PRP therapy involves drawing a small amount of your blood, spinning it in a special machine to concentrate the platelets and growth factors, and then injecting that enriched solution into thinning areas of your scalp.

These platelets help strengthen weak hair roots, reduce hair fall, and promote thicker hair growth.

It’s a completely non-surgical treatment, ideal for both men and women who are in the early stages of hair thinning.

## FAQs – PRP Hair Treatment in Bangalore

### Is PRP therapy safe for hair?

Yes, it’s completely safe. PRP uses your own blood, so there’s no risk of allergies or side effects. It’s a natural, low-risk way to stimulate hair growth.

### How many sessions will I need for visible results?

Most people see results after 3 to 4 sessions, spaced about 3–4 weeks apart. Some may require maintenance sessions once every 6–12 months.

### Does PRP hair treatment hurt?

You might feel slight pinching or pressure during the injections, but it’s not considered painful. Numbing cream can be used if needed.

### When will I start seeing results?

You may see reduced hair fall in 3–4 weeks, and visible hair regrowth in 2–3 months, with continued improvement in thickness and texture over time.

### When will I start seeing new hair growth?

You may notice reduced hair fall within a few weeks. Visible hair regrowth and improvement in thickness usually appear after 2–3 months.

### Can PRP help with bald spots?

PRP works best on thinning areas with active follicles. It may not be very effective in completely bald areas—those may need a hair transplant instead.

### Are there any side effects of PRP therapy?

Mild redness or tenderness in the scalp may occur, but this usually subsides in a few hours. Since it’s your own blood, the treatment is generally very safe.

### Is PRP suitable for women?

Absolutely! Many women benefit from PRP, especially those with postpartum hair loss, hormonal changes, or general thinning.

### Can I wash or style my hair after PRP?

You can wash your hair after 24 hours and resume normal styling. Just avoid chemical treatments for a few days after each session.
