Yes. We specialize in hairline redesigns to make them look natural and age-appropriate. Is there a waiting period before repair can be done? Ideally, you should wait 6 to 12 months after your initial transplant. This allows your scalp to heal and gives us a clear view of what worked, what didn’t, and what needs to be corrected. Can the same donor area be used again? It depends on how much donor hair is available. We assess this during your consultation. Will the repair results look natural? Yes, definitely. One of the most common goals in repair is to recreate a natural-looking hairline that suits your face, age, and hair type. We specialize in correcting unnatural or overly aggressive hairlines. Will I need to remove previously implanted grafts? Not always, but if the old grafts are placed poorly or are too noticeable, we may remove or reposition them for a better cosmetic outcome. This is discussed in your personalized plan. Can you fix visible scars from FUT or older techniques? In many cases, yes. We use techniques like scalp micropigmentation or strategic graft placement to minimize scar visibility. Have more questions? Let’s talk one-on-one.
