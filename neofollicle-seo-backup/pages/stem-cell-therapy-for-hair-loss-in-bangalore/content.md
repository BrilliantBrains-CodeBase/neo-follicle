## What Is Stem Cell Therapy for Hair Loss?

#### Your Body Already Has What It Takes to Regrow Hair

Stem cell therapy taps into your own regenerative power to kickstart hair growth in thinning or weak areas of the scalp.

![Hair loss treatment before after](https://neofollicletransplant.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Hair-Loss-Treatment-Before-After.jpg)

Stem cell therapy is a revolutionary treatment that uses the regenerative power of stem cells to promote hair growth.

Stem cells, unique cells in the body capable of transforming into different types of cells, play a crucial role in repairing and regenerating damaged tissues.

When applied to hair follicles, these cells can stimulate hair regrowth by promoting the production of new, healthy hair follicles.

The process involves extracting stem cells from your body, typically from areas such as bone marrow or fat tissue, and injecting them into the scalp.

These stem cells work to repair damaged hair follicles, reverse the miniaturization process (shrinking of hair follicles), and encourage the growth of new hair.

## FAQs – Stem Cell Therapy for Hair Loss in Bangalore

### Is stem cell therapy safe for hair loss?

Yes, absolutely. Since it uses your own biological material, the risk of side effects or allergic reaction is extremely low. It’s a well-tolerated and safe procedure.

### How many sessions will I need?

Most people need between 3 to 6 sessions, spaced 3–4 weeks apart. The exact number depends on your hair loss stage and how well you respond to the treatment.

### How soon can I see results?

You may notice reduced hair fall and better hair texture in 4–6 weeks. Visible new hair growth typically begins in 2–3 months.

### Does the procedure hurt?

You may feel mild discomfort or a pinching sensation during the injections, but it’s not considered painful. No cuts, no surgery, no stitches.

### Can it replace a hair transplant?

Not exactly. It’s best for mild to moderate hair thinning. If you have large bald patches or advanced hair loss, a transplant may be a better solution.

### Will I need maintenance sessions?

Yes, for best long-term results. Many patients opt for 1–2 maintenance sessions per year after their initial course.

### Can women benefit from this therapy too?

Absolutely! In fact, stem cell therapy is a popular, non-surgical option for women dealing with postpartum hair loss, hormonal thinning, or patterned hair fall.
