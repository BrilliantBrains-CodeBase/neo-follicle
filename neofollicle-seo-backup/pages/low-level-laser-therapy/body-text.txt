Not at all! The treatment is completely painless. Most people feel a mild warming or tingling sensation, but it’s relaxing and comfortable. How soon will I see results? Most patients start noticing reduced hair fall within 4–6 weeks, with new hair growth and improved thickness after 3–4 months of consistent sessions. How many sessions do I need? Just like any ongoing therapy, results can decline if you stop completely. We usually suggest maintenance sessions to keep hair follicles active. Is it safe for people with sensitive scalps?
