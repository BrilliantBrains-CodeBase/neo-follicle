## Types of Low-Level Laser Therapy

There are several techniques used in Low-Level Laser Therapy (LLLT), Here are some of the most common types:

✅ **Laser Combs:** Handheld devices that resemble a comb, which you use to brush through your hair while the lasers stimulate the scalp. They are designed for home use and are convenient for daily treatments.

✅ **Laser Helmets:** Wearable helmets equipped with lasers that cover the entire scalp. These devices provide a comprehensive treatment area and are often used for a set duration, typically a few times a week.

✅ **Laser Caps:** Similar to helmets, these caps are fitted with lasers and worn on the head. They are designed for ease of use and can be worn while engaging in other activities.

✅ **Laser Devices in Clinics:** Professional-grade LLLT devices used in clinics that provide more powerful and focused treatments.

## FAQs – Low Level Laser Therapy for Hair Loss in Bangalore

### Is LLLT painful or uncomfortable?

Not at all! The treatment is completely painless. Most people feel a mild warming or tingling sensation, but it’s relaxing and comfortable.

### How soon will I see results?

Most patients start noticing reduced hair fall within 4–6 weeks, with new hair growth and improved thickness after 3–4 months of consistent sessions.

### How many sessions do I need?

We usually recommend 2–3 sessions per week for at least 3 months, followed by maintenance treatments once a week or biweekly.

### Can LLLT be combined with other treatments?

Absolutely! LLLT works well when combined with PRP, GFC, stem cell therapy, or even post-hair transplant to accelerate growth and healing.

### Is LLLT safe for women?

Yes! LLLT is very effective for women, especially for postpartum, hormonal, or pattern-related hair thinning.

### Will I lose hair again if I stop LLLT?

Just like any ongoing therapy, results can decline if you stop completely. We usually suggest maintenance sessions to keep hair follicles active.

### Is it safe for people with sensitive scalps?

Yes, LLLT is gentle and does not irritate the scalp. It’s great for people who want a non-chemical, non-invasive hair regrowth solution.
