## What is Growth Factor Concentrate (GFC) Hair Therapy?

#### A Natural, Non-Surgical Solution That Uses Your Body’s Healing Power

Growth Factor Concentrate (GFC) Hair Therapy is a cutting-edge, non-surgical treatment that uses the body’s own natural healing proteins to stimulate hair growth, reduce hair fall, and rejuvenate thinning areas of the scalp.

Play

Unlike regular PRP therapy, GFC uses a special lab-based process to extract only the purest and most potent growth factors from your blood—leaving behind unnecessary components like red blood cells and plasma.

These concentrated growth proteins are then injected into areas where your hair is thinning or weakening, activating dormant follicles, improving blood flow, and promoting healthier, denser hair.

The treatment is completely safe, minimally invasive, and suitable for both men and women in the early to moderate stages of hair loss.

Many patients prefer GFC because it requires fewer sessions than traditional PRP, offers stronger results, and fits easily into a busy lifestyle with no downtime.

## FAQs – Growth Factor Concentrate Hair Treatment in Bangalore

### How is GFC different from PRP hair treatment?

GFC is a more advanced form of PRP. It separates only the most concentrated growth factors from your blood, resulting in a more powerful solution that requires fewer sessions and delivers faster, more consistent hair growth.

### Is GFC safe and does it have side effects?

Yes, it’s completely safe since it uses your own blood. There are no allergic reactions, and side effects are minimal—maybe slight redness or tenderness at the injection site, which usually fades in a few hours.

### How many GFC sessions will I need?

Most patients need 3 to 4 sessions, spaced 3–4 weeks apart. Some opt for maintenance sessions every 6–12 months for long-term support.

### When will I start seeing results?

You may see reduced hair fall in 3–4 weeks, and visible hair regrowth in 2–3 months, with continued improvement in thickness and texture over time.

### Can GFC work for both men and women?

Absolutely! GFC works well for both men and women experiencing hair thinning, shedding, or weak hair roots. It’s a gentle yet powerful solution for all genders.

### Will GFC help with bald spots?

GFC works best on areas with active or weak follicles. It’s not ideal for completely bald patches where follicles are no longer active. In such cases, a hair transplant might be needed.

### Is there any downtime after GFC therapy?

None at all. You can return to work or daily activities immediately after the procedure. Just avoid washing your hair or using styling products for 24 hours.

### Can I combine GFC with other treatments like PRP or transplant?

Yes! Many patients combine GFC with PRP, mesotherapy, or use it post-transplant to support healing and enhance final results.

### What are the growth factors associated with GFC therapy?

GFC therapy works by using a high concentration of natural proteins called growth factors that are extracted from your own blood. These include PDGF (Platelet-Derived Growth Factor), VEGF (Vascular Endothelial Growth Factor), EGF (Epidermal Growth Factor), and IGF (Insulin-like Growth Factor). Together, they help improve blood circulation, stimulate hair follicle activity, reduce inflammation, and create a healthier scalp environment for stronger, thicker hair regrowth—all without using any chemicals or medications.
