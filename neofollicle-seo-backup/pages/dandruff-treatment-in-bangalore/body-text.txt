What is the main cause of dandruff? Dandruff can be caused by seborrheic dermatitis, fungal infections, dry scalp, or product buildup. Yes, persistent dandruff can weaken hair roots, cause scalp inflammation, and lead to hair thinning or fall if left untreated. How many sessions do I need?
