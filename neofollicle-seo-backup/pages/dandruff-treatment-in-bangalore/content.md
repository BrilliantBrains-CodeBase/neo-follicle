## FAQs – Best Dandruff Treatment in Bangalore

### Why won’t my dandruff go away with regular shampoos?

Because over-the-counter shampoos only manage surface symptoms. If your dandruff is caused by a fungus, oily scalp, or inflammation, it needs targeted clinical treatment to get long-lasting relief.

### What is the main cause of dandruff?

Dandruff can be caused by seborrheic dermatitis, fungal infections, dry scalp, or product buildup. Our diagnostic process helps identify the exact reason in your case.

### Can dandruff cause hair loss?

Yes, persistent dandruff can weaken hair roots, cause scalp inflammation, and lead to hair thinning or fall if left untreated.

### How many sessions do I need?

Most patients feel better after 1–2 sessions, but we typically recommend a series of 3–6 treatments along with home care to fully control the condition.

### Is the treatment safe for color-treated or chemically straightened hair?

Absolutely. We use gentle, sulfate-free products that are safe for treated hair and won’t damage your hair color or texture.

### Do I need to keep coming forever for dandruff treatments?

Not at all. Once your condition is under control, we guide you on easy-to-follow home care to maintain a healthy scalp.

### Can men and women both get treated?

Yes! Our treatments are customized to individual needs and are suitable for all genders, hair types, and ages.

### Is dandruff contagious?

No, dandruff isn’t contagious. But if it’s caused by a fungal issue like Malassezia, it may spread on your own scalp without proper care.
