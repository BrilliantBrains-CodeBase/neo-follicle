Why Hair Transplant Cost Is Different for Every Patient

Hair transplant pricing is not the same for everyone because every person has a different pattern of hair loss, donor hair availability, and restoration goal.

At Neo Follicle Transplant Clinic, we do not recommend a one-size-fits-all package. Your cost is estimated after a detailed scalp and donor area assessment, so the treatment plan is designed for natural-looking results, safe donor usage, and long-term value.

1. Number of Grafts Required

The more grafts needed to cover the thinning or bald area, the higher the overall cost may be.

2. Grade of Baldness

Early hairline recession usually needs fewer grafts, while advanced baldness or crown thinning may need a larger graft plan.

3. Donor Area Quality

A strong donor area allows better planning. If donor hair is limited, the treatment strategy has to be more careful.

4. Hairline Design

A natural-looking hairline needs proper angle, direction, density, and artistic planning.

5. Technique Used

FUE, implant pen technique, NFT Method, beard transplant, and repair procedures may have different cost structures.

6. Density Expectations

Some patients need basic coverage, while others want higher density. This can change the number of grafts required.
Important: A lower quote may not always mean better value. Hair transplant is a medical and aesthetic procedure where planning, safety, surgeon expertise, and natural-looking results matter as much as cost.

FAQs - Hair Transplant Cost in Bangalore

How is the cost of a hair transplant calculated?

The cost is usually calculated based on the number of grafts you need and the technique used (FUE, FUT, or NFT). After a scalp evaluation, we can provide a personalized estimate.

How many grafts do I need, and how does that impact the price?

The number of grafts depends on the extent of your hair loss and desired coverage. For example, a receding hairline may need 1200–1800 grafts, while full crown restoration may require 2500+. More grafts = higher cost.

Is there a fixed package, or is every case priced differently?

At Neo Follicle, we offer both. You can choose between personalized per-graft pricing or bundled packages that include PRP sessions, follow-ups, and medication.

Are there any hidden charges apart from the quoted cost?

No. Our pricing is fully transparent. We include consultation, graft extraction, implantation, surgeon fees, post-op care, and your recovery kit in the final estimate.

Do you offer EMI or financing options for the procedure?

Yes! We provide easy EMI plans with 0% interest through trusted finance partners. You can spread the cost over several months based on eligibility.

Why do prices vary so much between clinics in Bangalore?

Prices depend on surgeon experience, technique, graft quality, safety protocols, and long-term results. Cheaper doesn’t always mean better—invest in results that last.

How does Neo Follicle’s pricing compare to other clinics?
