Beard Transplant

Fill patchy beard areas and improve beard shape and density.

Eyebrow Restoration

Restore eyebrow shape, fullness, and symmetry with precise follicle placement.

Eyebrow restoration is useful for people with thin eyebrows, over-plucked eyebrows, scars, or eyebrow hair loss. The procedure requires fine planning because eyebrow hair direction, angle, and density are very different from scalp hair.

Are You the Right Candidate for Hair Transplant?

Hair transplant is suitable only when the donor area, hair loss pattern, and expectations are medically appropriate.

You may be a suitable candidate for hair transplant if you have:

  • Receding hairline
  • Male pattern baldness
  • Crown baldness
  • Stable hair loss
  • Good donor hair at the back or sides of the scalp
  • Patchy beard or moustache growth
  • Thin or missing eyebrows
  • Hair loss after injury, scar, or previous surgery
  • Unsatisfactory result from an earlier hair transplant

However, hair transplant may not be the first option for every patient. If your hair loss is active, diffuse, medically driven, or temporary, the doctor may first recommend medical or non-surgical hair loss treatment.

Natural-Looking Results Begin With Realistic Planning

Hair transplant results depend on donor hair quality, graft survival, hair loss stage, and long-term maintenance.
A good hair transplant should not look artificial. The transplanted hair should blend with your existing hair and match the natural direction, angle, and pattern of growth.

At Neo Follicle, we help patients understand the complete result journey. Hair transplant results take time. Initial shedding can happen after the procedure. New growth usually appears gradually, and density improves over several months.

The final result depends on many factors, including:

  • Number of grafts implanted
  • Quality of donor hair
  • Hair thickness and texture
  • Scalp health
  • Grade of baldness
  • Graft survival
  • Post-procedure care
  • Future hair loss control
