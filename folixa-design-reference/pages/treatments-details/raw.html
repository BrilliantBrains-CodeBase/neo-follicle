Precision Meets Artistry

Follicular Unit Extraction (FUE) is the gold standard in hair restoration. Unlike traditional methods, FUE involves extracting individual hair follicles from a donor area and transplanting them into thinning regions, leaving no linear scars and ensuring a seamless, dense finish.

The FUE Journey

A meticulous three-step process for optimal growth. Grafts are carefully sorted and prepared under high-powered microscopes to ensure maximum viability before implantation.

Extraction

Individual follicles are gently harvested from the donor area using specialized micro-tools, ensuring minimal tissue disruption.

Preparation

Grafts are carefully sorted and prepared under high-powered microscopes to ensure maximum viability before implantation.

Implantation

Artistically placed following your natural hair growth pattern to achieve the desired density and hairline shape.

Key Advantages

  • Virtually No Visible Scarring
  • Rapid Recovery Time
  • Permanent, Lifetime Results
  • Natural Hairline Design

What to Expect

Procedure Time

Typically 4-8 hours depending on graft count. Local anesthesia is used for comfort.

Recovery

Most clients return to office work within 3-5 days. Full activity resumes in 14 days.

Timeline

New growth starts at 3 months, with full visible results appearing between 9-12 months.
