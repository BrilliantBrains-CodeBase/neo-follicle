## Answers About Hair

Get quick answers to common questions about hair transplant procedures, recovery time, results, and aftercare.

01\. Consultation

###### Understanding Your Hair Condition

We begin with a detailed consultation to assess hair loss, donor area strength, and the best treatment approach for natural results.

- Typically 30 to 45 minutes

02\. Procedure Details

###### What Happens During Treatment

The transplant process includes graft extraction, hairline planning, and precise implantation for balanced and natural-looking growth.

- Usually completed in 1 day

03\. Recovery Process

###### Healing After the Procedure

Most patients return to normal activities quickly, with proper aftercare guidance provided for smooth healing and better growth.

- Basic recovery in 3 to 7 days

04\. Final Results

###### When You See New Growth

Hair growth starts gradually after a few months, with stronger and fuller results becoming visible over time.

- Visible progress from 3 to 4 months
